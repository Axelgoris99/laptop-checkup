Webcam test

Check your camera works and see its real resolution and frame rate. The video stays on your screen: nothing is recorded or uploaded.

Buying a used laptop? The webcam is one of 17 checks in the free Laptop Checkup: keyboard, screen, battery wear, company locks and more, with a report you can copy at the end.

How to use it

  1. Press Start camera and allow camera access when the browser asks.
  2. If the laptop has more than one camera (front, infrared, external), pick it from the list.
  3. Check the image, then press Stop.

What to look for

  • Sharp image: text held up to the camera should be readable. Blur or a hazy spot can mean a scratched lens or moisture.
  • Natural colors and no dark or dead areas.
  • Camera light turns on while the camera runs. If it doesn't, the LED is broken.
  • Privacy shutter or switch, if the laptop has one, opens and closes properly.
  • Resolution matches what the seller advertised: 1280 × 720 is 720p, 1920 × 1080 is 1080p.

Questions

It says NotAllowedError

Camera access was refused. Click the camera icon in the address bar, allow it, and try again. On Windows, also check Settings → Privacy & security → Camera.

It says NotReadableError, or the image is black

Another app (Teams, Zoom, Discord) is using the camera, or a privacy shutter, switch or Fn key has turned it off. Close the other apps and check the shutter.

No camera found

Windows doesn't see a camera at all: the driver is missing, it's disabled in the BIOS or Device Manager, or the camera is disconnected inside. On a used laptop, ask the seller before you pay.
